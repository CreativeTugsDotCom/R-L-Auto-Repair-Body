Our Services & Repairs

From routine preventative maintenance to complex engine diagnostics and light body work, R&L handles your vehicle with professional precision.

General Auto Repair

Complete bumper-to-bumper steering, suspension, brake system, belt replacements, and cooling system fixes.

Engine & Mechanical Repair

Engine rebuilds, cylinder head repairs, timing belts, water pumps, fuel system service, and leak repair.

Transmission Service

Automatic and manual transmission repairs, fluid flushes, clutch replacements, and torque converter service.

Electrical Diagnostics

Check engine light scans, starter and alternator replacements, wiring fixes, battery testing, and sensors.

Oil Changes & Maintenance

Synthetic & conventional oil changes, filter changes, tire rotations, fluid top-offs, and multi-point inspections.

Light Body Work

Minor dent repair, panel alignment, bumper fixes, touch-up paint, trim restoration, and scratch repair.

Headlight & Lighting Repair

Oxidized headlight restoration, LED upgrades, lens replacements, fog light repair, and brake light wiring.

Domestic & Import Vehicles

Specialized care for Ford, Chevy, Dodge, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, BMW, Mercedes, and more.
